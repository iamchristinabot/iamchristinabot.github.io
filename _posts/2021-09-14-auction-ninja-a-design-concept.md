I've been working on a design concept called
[Auction Ninja](/projects/auction-ninja), an online marketplace for estate
sales and auctions, from browsing featured luxury lots through checkout.

Concept work is a different muscle than client work. There's no stakeholder
handing you constraints, so you have to invent honest ones: who is this buyer,
what do they need to trust before they bid, what does an auction listing have
to communicate at a glance? Estate sales turned out to be a fun design
problem. The inventory is one of a kind, the photography varies wildly, and
urgency is built in because every listing ends.

The pieces I enjoyed most were the listing cards, which have to make a
diamond ring and a duck decoy feel like part of the same marketplace, and the
balance between editorial warmth and utility in the browsing experience.

The full concept is on the [project page](/projects/auction-ninja).
