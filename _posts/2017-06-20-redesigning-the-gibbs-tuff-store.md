I recently wrapped up a redesign for [Gibbs Tuff](/projects/gibbs-tuff), a
retailer of Gibbs Brand Lubricant. If you're not familiar with it, it's a
product with a devoted following: it eliminates corrosion, waterproofs, and
lubricates just about any mechanical hardware.

The project covered the whole site, including a redesign of the store and the
shopping cart system. E-commerce work is a good reminder that design is never
just about how things look. Every decision between the product page and the
confirmation screen either helps someone finish their purchase or gives them a
reason to leave.

Working with a small business is also a different kind of collaboration than
working inside a larger company. The feedback loop is short, the priorities
are practical, and you can feel the impact of the work quickly.

You can see the finished work on the [project page](/projects/gibbs-tuff).
