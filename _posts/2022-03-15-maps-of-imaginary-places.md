For a while now I've had a graphic design side project called
[Maps of Imaginary Places](https://www.mapsofimaginaryplaces.com/) — a series
of geometric experiments exploring how colors and shapes interact. Each piece
is a visual journey through an abstract landscape that exists only in
imagination, which is where the name comes from.

<img src="/images/maps-of-imaginary-places/geometric-star-print.jpg" class="img-responsive" alt="Framed geometric star print with overlapping rainbow triangles"/>

The work is all clean lines and symmetry: kaleidoscopes, mountain ranges,
stripes, and the occasional piece of typography. Overlapping transparent
shapes do most of the heavy lifting — it's amazing how much depth you can get
from a few polygons and some restraint.

<img src="/images/maps-of-imaginary-places/lets-play-pretend.jpg" class="img-responsive" alt="Lets Play Pretend typography print in overlapping pastel letters"/>

The designs are available as prints, pillows, and other products in the
[shop](https://www.redbubble.com/people/imaginaryplaces/shop). Seeing a
pattern that started as flat geometry wrapped around a physical object never
gets old.

<img src="/images/maps-of-imaginary-places/color-prism-pillow.jpg" class="img-responsive" alt="Throw pillow with intersecting rainbow prism pattern"/>
<img src="/images/maps-of-imaginary-places/rainbow-stripes-pillow.jpg" class="img-responsive" alt="Throw pillow with diagonal rainbow stripes"/>

If you want to follow along, I post new pieces and behind-the-scenes glimpses
on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mapsimaginaryplaces/) and
[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/mapsofimaginaryplaces), and everything
lives at [mapsofimaginaryplaces.com](https://www.mapsofimaginaryplaces.com/).
