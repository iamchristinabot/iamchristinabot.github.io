Zebbly Wishlists

Zebbly is a social shopping site I took from idea to live product: create wish lists, add products from any store, and share them with the people who need them.

You can search Amazon, Target, and Walmart directly on the site, save products from any online store, and mix in custom ideas like subscriptions, charity donations, or local shopping. Lists carry context, like sizes, interests, and notes, so gift-givers aren't guessing.

Zebbly is live today, and our family wish lists run on it. I wrote about what building it alone taught me on the blog.
