For a while now I've been building [Zebbly](/projects/zebbly/), a social
shopping and wishlist site, and it's finally at the point where I can share
it. The idea is simple: create wish lists and share products you love, from
any store, in one place.

The itch came from how scattered wish lists are. Your Amazon list can't hold
the thing you found at a local shop, and "what does your daughter actually
want for her birthday" still gets answered over text message. Zebbly makes
the list itself the product. You can search Amazon, Target, and Walmart right
on the site, save products from anywhere else, and mix in custom ideas like
subscriptions or local shopping. Lists carry context, like sizes and
interests, so gift-givers aren't guessing.

Building a product alone is a strange and useful experience. There's no one
to hand the design to, no one to write the requirements for, and no one else
to blame when a feature doesn't earn its place. Every decision from the data
model to the empty states is yours, which is equal parts freeing and
humbling.

It's live now, and our family wish lists run on it. You can read more on the
[project page](/projects/zebbly/).
