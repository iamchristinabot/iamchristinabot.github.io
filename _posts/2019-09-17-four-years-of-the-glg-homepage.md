The [GLG website](/projects/glg-homepage) I redesigned and developed is being
retired this year, which feels like the right moment to look back on it. It
went live in 2015, which means it served as the public face of the company for
four years. In website years, that's a long and healthy life.

The interesting constraint on this project was that the branding already
existed. I wasn't inventing a visual identity; I was translating one into a
living site, page by page, from the homepage through careers. There's a
particular discipline to that kind of work. The design decisions aren't about
self-expression, they're about consistency and craft in service of a brand
that other people own.

Building something that lasts four years in production also teaches you
things that launches don't. Content changes, teams change, campaigns come and
go, and the structure you chose either bends gracefully or it doesn't.
